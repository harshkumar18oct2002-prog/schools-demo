Four decades of building character, on Bailey Road

Founded in 1985, Patna Public School has grown from three classrooms to a 42-acre campus — without losing sight of why it began.

Our Story

Started by five teachers who believed Patna deserved better

In 1985, five educators rented a small bungalow off Bailey Road with forty students and a shared conviction: that a rigorous education and a kind classroom were not mutually exclusive. That founding class has since grown into a school of over 2,400 students across Nursery to Class XII.

What hasn't changed is the belief that drives us — every child learns differently, and a school's job is to notice how, not to make them fit a single mould. That principle now shapes everything from our teacher training to our timetable.

Our Mission

To deliver a rigorous, CBSE-aligned education within a classroom culture of curiosity, respect and resilience — so every student leaves prepared for competitive exams and for the choices life asks of them.

Our Vision

To be Bihar's most trusted school — recognised as much for the character of its graduates as for their board results and college placements.

Principal's Message

“Marks fade. Character stays.”

When parents walk into my office during admissions season, they almost always ask about our board results first. It's a fair question, and I answer it honestly — but I always add a second answer they didn't ask for: what will your child be like as a person when they leave us?

That second question is the one this school has organised itself around for close to forty years. Our teachers are trained to notice the quiet child who needs drawing out and the loud one who needs channelling. Our timetable leaves room for failure — in a debate lost, a match not won, a science-fair project that didn't work — because we think resilience is taught in exactly those moments, not around them.

I invite you to visit our campus, sit in on a class, and see this philosophy at work before you decide. That's a better way to choose a school than any prospectus, including this one.

Dr. Meera Sinha

Principal, Patna Public School · M.Ed, Ph.D (Education)

Life on Campus

A campus that supports the whole child

Science & Robotics Labs

Individual lab stations for Physics, Chemistry and Biology from Class VI, plus a dedicated robotics and coding lab.

Central Library

Over 18,000 titles across fiction, reference and competitive-exam prep, plus a digital reading room.

Sports Arena

400m athletics track, cricket ground, basketball and badminton courts, and a heated swimming pool.

Art, Music & Dance

Purpose-built studios for fine art, classical and western music, and Kathak and contemporary dance.

Health & Wellness

An on-site nurse and visiting doctor, plus a counsellor available to every student, every day.

Transport

A GPS-tracked fleet of 70+ buses covering every corner of Patna, from Danapur to Kankarbagh.

Why Families Choose Us

Five reasons parents stay for all thirteen years

Low student-teacher ratio — 1:18 on average, so no child goes unnoticed.
Continuity of care — most homeroom teachers stay with a class across two academic years.
Transparent communication — a parent app with real-time attendance, grades and bus tracking.
Consistent board results — a 98% first-division rate across the last five CBSE Class X and XII cohorts.
A genuine alumni network — graduates return each year to mentor current students.

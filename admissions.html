Nursery through Class XI. A simple, transparent process — from enquiry to enrolment in five steps.

How It Works

Five steps to a seat at PPS

1. Submit Enquiry

Fill the form below or call the admissions office.

2. Document Review

Share birth certificate and previous report card, if applicable.

3. Interaction

An informal meeting with the child and parents — not a formal test.

4. Offer of Seat

Admission offers are sent within 5 working days.

5. Confirm Enrolment

Complete the fee payment to secure your child's seat.

Eligibility
  • Nursery: child must turn 3 years by 31st March of the academic year.
  • Class I and above: subject to seat availability and an informal interaction.
  • Transfer students require a transfer certificate from their previous school.
Fee Structure

Detailed, class-wise fee structures are shared directly with families after the initial interaction, so figures stay accurate for the year your child is joining. Call our admissions office at +91 98760 12345 for the current fee schedule.

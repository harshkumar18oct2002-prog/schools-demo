A CBSE curriculum, taught by people who stay curious

From foundational literacy in the primary wing to specialised streams in Classes XI–XII, our academic plan is built one stage at a time.

Curriculum

Four stages, one continuous journey

Nursery – Class II
Foundational Stage

Play-based learning, phonics and motor-skill development in small, nurturing classrooms.

Class III – V
Preparatory Stage

Structured literacy and numeracy, introduction to EVS, computing and a second language.

Class VI – X
Middle & Secondary

Full CBSE subject range with lab sciences, coding, and board-exam preparation from Class IX.

Class XI – XII
Senior Secondary

Science, Commerce and Humanities streams with dedicated competitive-exam mentoring.

Senior Secondary Streams

Choose a path, keep every door open

Our stream counsellors work with every Class X student individually before they choose — no stream is treated as the "default" option.

Science

PCM & PCB combinations, with dedicated JEE/NEET mentoring hours.

Commerce

Accountancy, Economics and Business Studies, with a CA/CS foundation club.

Humanities

Political Science, History, Psychology and Economics for the civil-services minded.

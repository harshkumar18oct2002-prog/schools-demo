Campus & Facilities

Spaces built for how children actually learn

From science labs to a full-size sports arena, every corner of our Bailey Road campus is designed with a purpose.

Science & Robotics Labs

Fully equipped Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Robotics labs for hands-on discovery.

Central Library

18,000+ titles, a digital reading corner, and a quiet reading courtyard.

Sports Arena

Athletics track, cricket ground, basketball and badminton courts, and a swimming pool.

Art & Music Studio

Dedicated studios for fine art, classical & western music, and dance.

Smart Classrooms

Interactive boards and connected classrooms from Class III onward.

Campus Safety

CCTV-monitored campus, GPS-tracked buses, and trained medical staff on-site.

Wholesome Cafeteria

Nutritionist-approved meals prepared fresh in an in-house kitchen.

Transport Network

70+ buses covering every corner of Patna, from Danapur to Kankarbagh.
